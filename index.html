03 / Plan before you claim

Incentives &
tax watch-outs.

Incentives depend on eligibility and how the business operates. The Playbook recommends planning the model first, then matching support to it.

Incentive snapshot PLAYBOOK · PP. 3, 11, 15

Build the model.
Then assess the support.

The Playbook lists a 5% corporate tax rate for qualifying manufacturing activities, with capital investment thresholds of RM500 million and RM1 billion (excluding land). It also describes a 5% personal income tax rate for knowledge workers.

Malaysia’s JS-SEZ incentives are zone-specific. Singapore companies may also consider grants and incentives from agencies such as EDB and EnterpriseSG. Requirements differ; check eligibility and ongoing conditions.

Keep in view

01

Where work happens

Frequent Johor visits for supervision, negotiations or decisions may create a permanent establishment and Malaysian tax obligations. Define roles and keep records.

PLAYBOOK · P. 14
02

Related-company pricing

Goods and services between Singapore and Johor entities should follow the arm’s length principle. Keep documentation for cross-border transactions.

PLAYBOOK · P. 14
03

Origin and customs data

Track supplier declarations, materials, processing and HS codes. Inaccurate origin declarations can put preferential tariff treatment at risk.

PLAYBOOK · P. 16
04

Staff workdays and passes

The JS-SEZ does not replace domestic tax or immigration rules. Track workdays, locations and activities, and keep records for treaty claims.

04 / Plan for your people

Passes for
staff and visitors.

The Playbook describes several Malaysian routes for expatriate employees, temporary specialists and business travellers. The right route depends on the work and the applicant.

EMPLOYMENT PASS · STAFF

For approved expatriate positions

MIDA assesses expatriate posts for manufacturing, selected service sectors and approved regional establishments or offices. A Key Post is a permanent high-level managerial role; a Term Post is temporary and tied to a defined period.

Category IRM 10,000
Category IIRM 5,000–9,999
Category IIIRM 3,000–4,999

The Playbook states these revised minimums take effect on 1 June 2026. Applications use the Single Window Platform via Xpats Gateway.

PLAYBOOK · P. 28

PROFESSIONAL VISIT PASS · TEMPORARY

For a specific professional assignment

For overseas-company professionals doing temporary work such as training, consultancy or project-based work. The pass is for up to 12 months and is not extendable.

Applications can be made through the ESD Online System and are evaluated by Malaysia’s Immigration Department.

PLAYBOOK · PP. 28–29

INVESTOR PASS · BUSINESS TRAVEL

For eligible foreign business travellers

Allows multiple entries under the existing Social Visit Pass. Stay may be up to 12 months: six months at a time, with a possible further six months subject to justification and requirements.

The Playbook lists new investors, investors in the pipeline and existing investors as the three categories. Apply through Xpats Gateway.

PLAYBOOK · P. 29

SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, pp. 28–29. Check the relevant criteria before applying.
